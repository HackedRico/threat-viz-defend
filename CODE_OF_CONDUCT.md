# Code of conduct

ThreatViz Defend follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and any event where someone represents the project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to threat modeling or to open source.
- Critique code and ideas, never the person who wrote them.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Accept a decision you disagree with once it is made, and say so if you want it revisited later.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, slurs or unwelcome sexual attention, in public or in private.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a thread.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or an email, without their permission.
- Posting exploit details for an unfixed vulnerability in a public issue. [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says how to report one privately.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior worries you, report it privately to the maintainer, [@HackedRico](https://github.com/HackedRico), through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Say what happened, where, and when, with links if you have them. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code or issues that break this code, and may warn, suspend or ban a contributor, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
